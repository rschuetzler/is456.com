# Lab 9 — Red-team swap

Status: outline, from the v0.3 schedule.

| | |
|---|---|
| When | Week 12, Thu Mar 25 |
| Who | Team; security lead |
| Tools | Any |

## What students do

- Attack another team's agent, including through a poisoned invoice or policy document. File findings.
- Turn the findings against your own agent into controls and new eval cases.
- Rerun the suite. Capstone 2 is submitted at the end of the day.

## What they should notice

- The attack that works is usually in a document, not the chat.
- A control that blocks the attack can also lower the score.
- Every finding becomes a permanent eval case.

A findings template; rules of engagement for the swap.
