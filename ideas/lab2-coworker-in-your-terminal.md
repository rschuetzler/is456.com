## What students do

- Set up Codex and choose an approval mode deliberately.
- Non-coding task: from a folder of Summit Ridge vendor-spend files, produce a short report, then check three of its numbers by hand.
- Write an `AGENTS.md` for the folder, rerun and compare.
- Label each step of the session as model, tool call or tool result, as in Lab 0.

## What they should notice

- The first report has at least one number worth questioning.
- Instructions in a file change behavior on every run.
- Every tool call is visible if you look.
