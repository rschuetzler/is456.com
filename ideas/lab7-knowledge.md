## What students do

- Build keyword, semantic and hybrid search over Summit Ridge policies and supplier contracts.
- Compare them on a labelled query set.
- Attach the best as a tool on the team's agent and rerun the scenarios.
- This is the fix promised in Lab 0, question 17.

## What they should notice

- Semantic search wins on paraphrase and loses on exact identifiers.
- A wrong answer can come from the right document.
- Better retrieval does not always raise the agent's score.
